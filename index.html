A tiny world where sound creates the terrain, while real-world time, weather, sunrise/sunset, and moonrise/moonset shape the sky.
Small device. Small world. BONSAI Spirit.

audio: demo
Tokyo, Japan
Press START MICROPHONE and allow microphone access in your browser. J = Jump, W = Wave, U = UFO, T = show/hide date, weekday, clock and weather. I = show/hide detailed information and the 8-band EQ: SUN/MOON rise/set, tide, TEMP/HUM, pressure, rain probability, STEP/AP/LOCATION and BAT. The EQ continues to drive the terrain even while hidden. The browser mirrors hardware-only fields with STEP xxxx, AP: xxxxxxxx and BAT xx% placeholders; no BMI270 step count, Wi-Fi AP state or Cardputer battery state is simulated.
