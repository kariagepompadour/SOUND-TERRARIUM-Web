Sound from the built-in microphone continuously shapes the terrain. Tilting the device left or right also tilts the terrain.
Device tilt control is available only on the Cardputer ADV version.

Press START MICROPHONE and allow microphone access in your browser. The EQ continues to drive the terrain even while hidden. The browser shows STEP xxxx as a placeholder and AP: BROWSER because browsers cannot read the connected Wi-Fi name. The old battery placeholder has been retired.
